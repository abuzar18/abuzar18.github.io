SAM BOT: Conversational BI and Text-to-SQL Assistant

A conversational BI service that turns plain-English questions over spreadsheets and databases into tables, charts and executive summaries, cutting manual analysis time by about 60%.

The problem

Business users waited on analysts for routine questions about their own data.

What I built

  • Built SAM BOT as a FastAPI service using Azure OpenAI and PandasAI.
  • Encoded business rules and definitions directly into the text-to-SQL prompts, so answers matched how the business measures things.
  • Returned results as tables, charts and short executive summaries rather than raw query output.

Result

Manual analysis time dropped by about 60%, and non-technical users could self-serve routine questions.
