Meeting-Intelligence Automation with Azure OpenAI and Salesforce

FastAPI services that schedule meeting bots, ingest recordings and transcripts, generate summaries and action items with Azure OpenAI, and sync them to Salesforce.

The problem

Meeting notes and follow-ups were written by hand and often never reached the CRM.

What I built

  • Built FastAPI services that schedule meeting bots and receive recording and transcript webhooks into S3.
  • Generated summaries and action items with Azure OpenAI and synced the results to Salesforce.
  • Hardened the pipeline with authentication, signed requests, deduplication, idempotency and replay-safe recovery.
  • Added structured logging, CloudWatch monitoring and per-request token, cost and latency telemetry.

Result

Meeting outcomes reached the CRM automatically, with a pipeline that tolerates retries and duplicate webhooks.
